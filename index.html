      One ridge, four climates.

      The lake house

      A boathouse of cedar and stone with a 25-metre indoor pool, a wood-fired kitchen and a sauna that opens straight onto the water.

      Forest trails

      Eleven kilometres of private paths, lit only at ankle height, so you can walk home under the stars without ever seeing a lamp post.

      The observatory

      A 14-inch telescope under a sliding roof. Lake Vale sits beneath one of the darkest skies in north India; Saturn's rings are an ordinary Tuesday.

      Residence care

      Housekeeping, a resident chef on request, and a team that airs the house, lights the fires and stocks the cellar before you arrive.
